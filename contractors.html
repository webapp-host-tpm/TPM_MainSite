Ways we can work together

Keep the scope clear. Build something useful for both sides.

Standard Project

A clearly defined video, website, or content project with an agreed scope, schedule, and price.

Service Credit

When it makes sense, some of your contractor services can be worked into the price. The remaining balance is paid normally.

Build It in Phases

Start with one useful project, see how we work together, and add more only when it is worthwhile.

Both sides price their work normally and agree on the scope before starting. Materials, disposal charges, permits, and other direct job costs stay separate unless we specifically agree otherwise.

Good marketing does not dress the work up. It helps people understand what really goes into it.
Why I do this

Helping small businesses be seen for the work they do.

I understand more than the finished result

I know what it means to work with your hands.

I grew up working on a farm, and I still run equipment, cut timber, mill lumber, build, and make repairs on my own property.

That does not make me the expert in your trade. It does mean I pay attention to why you approach a job a certain way—the planning, equipment, experience, and decisions most customers never see.
