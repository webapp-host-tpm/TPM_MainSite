The difference

A creative studio with an engineer’s mindset.

Ten Peak Media is a full-service video production, social media, and web design company founded by Adam Burger.

Adam’s background in civil engineering and visual communications brings a practical, problem-solving point of view to creative work. That combination helps the studio go beyond making something that looks good: every project is organized around the audience, the message, and the result.

Raised in a small northeast Ohio town and shaped by early experiences working on a farm, Adam has always respected people who build things, solve problems, and take pride in their craft. That is why local businesses and community stories remain at the heart of the work.

How we work

Listen deeply. Find the human angle. Make it clear.

  • Start with the real communication goal
  • Make interviews comfortable and conversational
  • Capture the details that reveal craftsmanship
  • Deliver useful assets, not just one finished video
We are most interested in the honest details—the hands, the place, the reason someone chose to do the work in the first place.
